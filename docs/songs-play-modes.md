Shuffle ModeShuffle mixes up the order of your music tracks so they play in a randomized sequence. Most apps use a smart algorithm that pre-shuffles the entire queue so you hear every song exactly once without hearing immediate repeats or the same track twice in a row.Smart Shuffle: Popularized by streaming platforms like Spotify Support, this mode mixes your chosen playlist with dynamically generated, personalized recommendations that match the vibe of your tracks.Repeat OptionsThe Repeat icon usually looks like one or two looping arrows. It dictates what happens when a song or playlist finishes playing.Repeat Off: The player stops playing music once the current queue or album reaches the final song.Repeat All (Loop): The music player will loop back to the first song and restart the current playlist or album from the beginning once it ends.Repeat One: This setting loops the exact same song over and over continuously until you manually change it. It is typically indicated by a small number "1" inside the repeat arrow icon.Queue ControlsThe Queue is the temporary list of upcoming songs scheduled to play next.Play Next (Add to Front): This inserts a selected song directly after the currently playing track, bypassing the rest of the existing queue order.Add to Queue (Add to End): This places a track at the very bottom of your list so it plays after all previously selected songs finish.Crossfade and Gapless PlaybackThese settings control the transitions between individual audio tracks to create a seamless listening experience.Crossfade: This fades out the ending of the current song while simultaneously fading in the beginning of the next track. You can usually adjust the fade duration in your app settings from 1 to 12 seconds.Gapless Playback: This removes the intentional 1 to 2-second silence between audio files. It is ideal for live concert recordings, concept albums, and continuous DJ mixes where tracks are meant to flow into each other perfectly
