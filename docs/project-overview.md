# MusiqSphere

## Tagline

Your Music Universe

## Overview

MusiqSphere is a cross-platform music streaming application that enables users to stream and download music across Android, iOS, and Web platforms. It focuses on personal music ownership while allowing global discovery.

Users can:

- Stream music online
- Download music for offline playback (Web & Mobile)
- Maintain a personal collection separate from the global library
- Clone songs from the global explorer to their personal library
- Create and manage playlists
- Like and favorite songs
- Search songs, artists, and albums (Global and Local scopes)
- Access their music library from multiple devices
- Manage uploaded songs (Edit metadata/Delete from cloud)
